# Interview Cheat Sheet
**Company:** Allstate

## Tell me about yourself (60–90 seconds)
I am a platform-oriented Product Manager with experience stabilizing data-heavy B2B systems, roadmap execution, and cross-functional delivery. This aligns with Allstate's focus on platform product delivery.

## Why this company / role?
Allstate is operating in a space where reliable product execution matters I have shipped comparable platform and data-integrity work and want to apply that pattern here.

## Proof stories (resume-backed)
1. Mitigated recurring indexing server crashes by implementing proactive storage capacity monitoring, alerting thresholds, and legal-approved cleanups of stale accounts, reducing overall service outages.
2. Conceived and drove a centralized platform data remediation initiative that bypassed failing legacy ETL paths to integrate directly with the upstream source-of-truth database.

## Handling ambiguity and stability
Frame legacy constraints as prioritization problems: protect revenue paths, sequence migrations, and ship incremental reliability wins with clear metrics.

## Reverse-interview questions
- How do you measure platform reliability and customer impact today?
- What is the biggest technical debt item on the roadmap this quarter?
- How does product partner with engineering on prioritization and tradeoffs?
