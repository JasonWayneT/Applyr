Job Description Summary
The Architecture Compute, and Engineering organization provides a transformative platform ecosystem with a comprehensive set of capabilities that empowers end users and digital product teams to innovate seamlessly and drive growth for Allstate through the Consumer Connected Cloud mission. This ecosystem aims to provide an absolute frictionless experience for our user community.  

As a Digital Product Manager in the ACE organization, you'll be contributing towards defining, delivering, and managing the lifecycle of one or more products ensuring they meet the needs of the customers and align with the company’s strategic goals. This role requires a blend of strategic thinking, technical understanding, and business acumen. 

A career as a Digital Product Manager is both challenging and rewarding, offering the opportunity to innovate and shape the future of digital products. Ideal candidates are strategic thinkers with strong technical and communication skills, capable of driving product success in a fast-paced environment.

There are a range of Digital Product Manager roles available at different levels.

Key responsibilities

Product Strategy and Vision: Develop and communicate the vision and strategy for digital product/s.  
Provide input on decomposing complex problems into smaller manageable tasks by creating and prioritizing a backlog for the Product Team.
Engage in discovery/test & learn activities to quickly validate assumptions and product direction.
Represent the “voice of the customer”; invest significant time with current and potential customers to understand their needs and habits.
Define and manage metrics to measure the success of your efforts and to inform the overall product strategy.
Authors, tests, and accepts stories.
Contribute to creating the product strategy for one or more products in our growing digital product portfolio.
Product Development, collaborate with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and launch new digital products
Create and continuously maintain a product roadmap that aligns with business goals and customer needs.  
Define product performance metrics & monitor product performance using analytics tools and make data-driven decisions. 

Essential criteria  

All candidates must have the legal right to work in the UK. Allstate is unable to provide sponsorship for this position.
Proven experience of at least 3+ years as a Digital Product Manager (Senior Consultant level) or 4+ years for the Digital Product Manager (Lead level) or 5+ years for the Digital Product Manager (Expert level).
Product experience in End User, Network, Compute, or Platform as a Service domain. 
Experience in technical areas such as cloud, networking, identity and access management, infrastructure as code, automation or data.
Proven delivery across multiple Agile release cycles, with hands-on use of tools (e.g., Jira, ADO) to manage backlogs, prioritisation, and delivery tracking.
Experience working with engineering, UX/UI, and business stakeholders to deliver 1–2+ product initiatives, including defining requirements or user stories.
Evidence of communicating product vision, roadmaps, or complex concepts to multi-level stakeholders, including leading demos, reviews, or roadmap sessions.
Proven use of product metrics and analytics to prioritise and improve products, including tracking KPIs and delivering incremental releases.

Additional Responsibilities for Lead and Expert Digital Product Manager

2+ years’ experience (Expert level) leading cross-functional, geographically distributed teams, with evidence of influencing delivery outcomes across product, engineering, and platform stakeholders
Proven experience influencing across product management peers within the product family, as well as across the product managing engineer & platform consultant  
Proven experience owning and executing product strategy and roadmap independently, including prioritising initiatives and delivering outcomes across multiple release cycles
Proven experience operating as a ‘coach’ partnering & mentoring early-in-trade product management peers across a broader technology organisation; a known expert in the organization 

Desirable Experience

Experience working in the insurance industry or with enterprise-level software solutions.

Supervisory Responsibilities

We are recruiting across multiple levels and roles. Some positions will include supervisory responsibilities, while others will not.
