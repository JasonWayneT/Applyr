# CR-133 — Hundreds of client databases

**Status:** Implemented
**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Requirements:** FR-393, AC-503

## Product outcome

A sentence that says hundreds of client databases is a hard block at Stage 1 and Stage 2. The block holds when the sentence cites a different fact, and when it cites nothing. A sentence that says roughly 200 SQL databases does not block.

## Why

MET-09 is roughly 200 SQL databases. The before-fix Classlink letter says hundreds of client databases and cites ACC-102 and ACC-121.

## Out of scope

- The same sentence's claim that customer support escalations were significantly reduced
- Changing the evaluator
