# CR-135 — Support escalation reduction

**Status:** Implemented
**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Requirements:** FR-395, AC-505

## Product outcome

A sentence that says support escalations were reduced is a hard block at Stage 1 and Stage 2. The block holds when the sentence cites a different fact, and when it cites nothing. A Jira priority formula that says streamline does not block. A sentence about stale-data complaints does not block.

## Why

Work experience does not use the word escalation. The before-fix Classlink letter says the data-remediation work significantly reduced customer support escalations and cites ACC-102.

## Out of scope

- "Streamline Customer Support escalations" on the real Jira priority formula
- "Guided release cadence" and "testing analytics"
- Changing the evaluator
